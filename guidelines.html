Community Guidelines

  • Respect your neighbors and handle all tools with care.
  • Return tools on the agreed date to avoid penalties.
  • Report any tool damage immediately to the owner.
  • Use tools only for their intended purpose.
  • Do not share borrowed tools with others without permission.
  • Follow all safety instructions provided with the tools.
  • Maintain honesty in transactions and payments.
  • ToolPool has zero tolerance for fraud or misuse.
